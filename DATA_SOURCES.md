# Data sources

Research checked 1 October 2026.

## GP Patient Survey 2026
Practice-level results are published by Ipsos/NHS England and include measures such as ease of contacting the practice by phone, website and NHS App.

Source: https://www.gp-patient.co.uk/latest-survey/results

## Patients Registered at a GP Practice - September 2026
NHS England publishes practice-level registered-patient totals.

Source: https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/publications/statistical/patients-registered-at-a-gp-practice/september-2026

## Healthtech-1 public product context
Bookable Search is described as supporting new-patient discovery/booking, while Bookable Navigator is described as handling existing-patient requests across form, text/WhatsApp and voice, using doctor-developed AI to route requests.

Source: https://www.healthtech1.uk/bookable

## Important limitation
Public datasets do not reveal Healthtech-1's actual customer list, account status, product usage, pricing, churn, ROI or internal feedback. Those layers are synthetic in this portfolio project.
