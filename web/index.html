01 / THE PRINCIPLE

The data tells you where to look.
It doesn't tell you what to do.

In a GP practice, low access scores are not a diagnosis. A training session is not adoption. A drafted support ticket is not a resolved problem. This exercise keeps those boundaries visible while showing how an operator might investigate and respond.

NOTE / EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Interesting work.
Clear limits.

Real: an independent working web application, official GP Patient Survey observations when accessible, validated classification rules and deterministic decisions.

Simulated: customer accounts, rollout states, training, usage, saved time, support feedback, proposed improvements and outcomes.

Not claimed: clinical data migration, real NHS deployments, supplier affiliation, patient-level data, verified commercial return or automated healthcare decisions.
