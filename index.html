01 / THE WORK

Fast answers.
Careful thinking.

The best first response isn't always the fastest guess. It's the one that moves the customer forward without losing the evidence.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Clarify before you fix.

Check the customer's report, affected workflow and diagnostic evidence. Separate observations from assumptions.

02 — OWN

Resolve or route well.

Fix routine problems. Give engineering, security or specialist colleagues a complete handover when necessary.

03 — IMPROVE

Make the next case easier.

Verify the outcome, communicate clearly and turn a repeatable answer into approved guidance.

03 / WHAT THIS IS

Independent work.
Explicit limits.

Real: working technical checks, locally persisted case history, evidence-backed escalation rules, reusable knowledge, and an optional server-side model connector.

Fictional: all firms, matters, messages, records, support outcomes and diagnostic environments. Nothing connects to Solve Intelligence or to real patent, customer or law-firm systems.

Human-owned: legal interpretations, incident management, external communications and final resolution decisions. AI suggestions are drafts, not completed actions.
